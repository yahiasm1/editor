Kobe Bryant once scored more points in one half than the entire Toronto Raptors team. January 22, 2006, and honestly, the night started terribly. The Lakers' starting five was Smush Parker, Kobe, Lamar Odom, Kwame Brown and Chris Mihm. Not exactly a super team.

At halftime the Raptors were up 63 to 49, and the home crowd was booing. Kobe had 26 points, which sounds like a lot, until you hear what came next. Early in the third quarter, the Lakers fell behind by 18. So Kobe just decided to win it himself.

Pull-up jumpers, threes, drives, free throws. Every time down the floor, it was him. By the end of the third quarter, the Lakers were in front. In the second half alone, Kobe scored 55 points. The whole Raptors team scored 41. One guy, against five.

He finished with 81 points on 28 of 46 shooting, and the Lakers won 122 to 104. That's the second most points in NBA history, behind only Wilt Chamberlain's 100.

And it wasn't even a one-off. A month earlier, Kobe outscored the entire Dallas Mavericks through three quarters, 62 to 61, and then sat on the bench for the fourth because the game was already over.
