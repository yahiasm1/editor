The Night Kobe Bryant Outscored the Whole Raptors Team in One Half

January 22, 2006. The Lakers started Smush Parker, Kobe, Lamar Odom, Kwame Brown and Chris Mihm against Toronto, and it went badly fast. Down 63 to 49 at halftime, the home crowd booing, and early in the third they were down 18.

So Kobe took over. Pull-up jumpers, threes, drives, free throws, every single trip down the floor. He scored 55 in the second half by himself. The entire Raptors team scored 41.

He finished with 81 points on 28 of 46 shooting and the Lakers won 122 to 104. Only Wilt Chamberlain's 100 is higher.

And a month before that, he outscored the whole Dallas Mavericks 62 to 61 through three quarters, then watched the fourth from the bench.
