## Sound-off test
A basketball floor fills with gold dots on real spots, each one feeding a board of gold bulbs, while a red count crawls;
then a list of plays where every line is the same name; the scoreboard turns from red ahead to Lakers ahead. A stranger
reads: one guy scored from everywhere, over and over, and that flipped the game.
