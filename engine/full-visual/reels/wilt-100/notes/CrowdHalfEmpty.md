# CrowdHalfEmpty (`crowd`, 12.12-22.02 s)

## What I saw (references at full size)

- `photos/commons-hershey-sports-arena-1938-interior-pd.jpg` (the angle of the scene): a high end view down a long
  oval floor. The roof is one concrete barrel vault: broad pale ribs (about 10 across the length) with darker recessed
  bays between them, springing straight off the top seat rows; small round vents dotted on the bays. Big round
  reflector lamps hang along the crown and throw a cone of light onto the floor. Seats rise steep on both long sides
  (about 25 rows), less steep at the far end; stairs cut the banks into sections every 12-15 seats, and boxy vomitory
  portals stand at mid height. At the far end, above the end seats, a hanging scoreboard with a clock and two side
  panels. Railing on top of low white boards around the floor.
- `photos/commons-hersheypark-arena-inside-2024-...` and `-inside-2007-pd.jpg`: the same vault today. The ribs are
  wide flat bands, the vault is a pointed-ish ellipse, the seat banks are wooden on the upper tier, broken into
  sections by stair aisles; the scoreboard and banners hang on the far end wall. The vault takes half the picture: it
  is the building.
- `objects/commons-rca-tk41-tv-camera-...jpg`: brushed silver box body, a raised hood on top with three rows of
  louvre slots, the black lens turret on the front with several lenses, a red script badge "Color Television" on the
  side, a chrome column through a big chrome steering ring, a grey bell base and a cast three-foot spider on castors.
  Cables coiled on the floor behind.
- `objects/commons-zenith-tube-radio-1954-...jpg`: ivory case, slightly trapezoidal, rounded edges, two square
  gold-framed dial windows. Left one a clock (cream face, 1-12, black hands, three small gold knobs under it); right
  one the broadcast band: cream ring with gold numerals 5.5 6 7 8 9 10 12 14 16 and "STANDARD BROADCAST" round the
  bottom, a pointer. The centre is a stepped rectangular grille with a small crown badge; "ZENITH" spaced along the
  base bar between two big gold knobs; two splayed feet.
- Typewriter photos for the press row: Commons answered 429 three times (rate limit, three reels fetching). The
  covered typewriter is drawn from what it is: a soft dark vinyl dome over a squat machine, the carriage bump on top,
  a seam round the hood, rubber feet showing under the hem.

What would give a fake away: a flat dome instead of ribs and bays; seats as an even grid without stair aisles;
dots that do not sit on seats; a modern camera; a radio without its two dials.

## The thinking

- The real thing: the inside of Hershey Sports Arena on the night, seen first from the high end (the 1938 angle),
  then down at the floor where the cameras and press would be, then up at the far end scoreboard. One canvas world in
  metres with one camera (vault, ribs, bays, vents, hanging lamps, 7,100 wooden seats in sections, the 1962 court with
  its 12 ft keyholes on the oval floor), and the period objects drawn as detailed sprites standing in that world.
- Details a viewer would miss only if gone: the stair aisles splitting the banks, the vomitories, lamp cones on the
  floor, the vents, the keyholes and baskets, the TK-41's louvres and lens turret, the radio's dial numerals, the chairs
  folded empty behind covered typewriters.
- Word, then change:
  - Only: the torn stub flies into the arena door; we follow it through and rise to the high end view.
  - 4,124 (lands): the turnstile counter rolls 0 to 4,124, and every number is one amber dot on one seat, filling from
    the floor up in clumps. The counter IS the number of dots.
  - showed up: the dots settle (people sitting down), a light drifts down the vault.
  - half (lands): every empty seat outlines grey at once; "empty" labelled on the top rows.
  - the building: the camera sinks toward the right sideline.
  - TV (lands): the RCA TK-41 stands on its riser at courtside, tally lamp dark; a red line strikes it.
  - cameras: its cable pulls out of the back and slides away, the riser sinks.
  - reporters (lands): the press table, typewriters covered, folding chairs empty; label "no New York press"; red
    strike along the table.
  - recording (lands): the Zenith at the end of the table, its broadcast dial glows amber (the only lit thing there).
  - radio (lands): amber waves out of the grille.
  - audio of the fourth: the camera rises to the far end scoreboard, dark, lamps 1 2 3 4 unlit; the waves travel up.
  - quarter (lands): lamp 4 lights amber, the waves arrive and run under it as a waveform; "the only recording".
- Sound-off test: a stranger sees a big old arena only half lit by amber people, a TV camera and a press row crossed
  out, and one old radio whose signal lights only the last quarter on the board. That is the sentence.
