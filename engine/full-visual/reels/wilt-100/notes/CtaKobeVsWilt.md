## The real thing

Two press prints of two nights on one table: the 1962 AP print (black and white, warm paper border, matte, a little
worn) and a 2006 colour print (glossy, cool white border). Each with its own flip counter under it. The creator's
Instagram follow row above.

## Details that make it real

- Print borders differ by era: 1962 cream #EFE6D2, softened corners, faint grain, a slight bow; 2006 bright white
  with a gloss sheen sweeping across.
- Backs first (face down on "story"): plain photo paper with pencil notes in Kalam, "Hershey, Pa. 1962" and
  "Los Angeles, 2006".
- Credits on the prints' bottom border in small grey: "AP photo by Paul Vathis, public domain", "endlessbender, CC BY 2.0".
- A hairline label points at #8: "Kobe".
- Kobe's counter runs his real quarter totals 14, 26, 53, 81.
- Amber (witness light) only on the 1962 print's edge once it is the one in front: it is a witness that kept the 100.
