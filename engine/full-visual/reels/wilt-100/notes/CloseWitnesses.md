# CloseWitnesses (`close`, 76.28-79.80 s)

"So yes, the greatest scoring night ever, and almost nobody saw it."

## What I saw (references at full size)

- `photos/commons-hershey-arena-placard-100-point-game-crazycat8590-cc0.jpg` (CC0, 2024): a Pennsylvania state
  historical marker on a navy round post in a parking lot, grey overcast sky. The plate is the standard PHMC shape:

  (readable on a 4x crop): "In a basketball game played here on March 2, 1962, Philadelphia Warriors center Wilt

  season, Chamberlain set other records by averaging 50.4 points per game and by scoring more than 50 points in each

  dark pines, a roller coaster's red track, SUVs, orange cones, a big boulder. Today, daylight, ordinary: the only

What would give a fake away: a dome instead of a barrel vault with ribs; a roof that just fades as one sheet (it must
open bay by bay between real ribs); a lit, busy arena (the point is how few saw it); witnesses lined up as cards; an
amber anywhere but on a witness; a marker with invented words.

## The thinking

- **The real thing:** the night, seen from outside, as evidence. The marker (the only thing on site today that
  remembers) is the door; behind it, the hook's frame: the vault at night from the Beato angle, the counter at 100
  above it. The shell is cut open the way the format cuts a phone open, and inside only four things light up: the
  people in the seats, the radio, the flashbulb, the ball. Then the camera rises until all of Hershey is one amber
  point on the dark state. It is the hook's sentence again, resolved: the counter still says 100, and the light
  that saw it is that small.
- **Details that make it real:** ribs as raised bands with a lit edge and a shadow edge, the eave fins, the four
  dormers, the two-storey windows (a few still warm, then out), the gable panels, the annex, the towers with red domes,
  trees, the car park lamps; inside, the oval floor with the 1962 court and keyholes, seat banks cut by stairs, 4,124
  amber dots on about 7,100 seats (the empty ones dim grey); the press table with the radio on the near sideline; the
  flash going off in the locker room under the end stands; the ball rolling out of the main gate onto the plaza; the
  Kiss lamps along Chocolate Avenue and the two stacks; the state outline with its ridges, the Susquehanna.
- **Word, then change:**
  - *So yes, the*: the marker photo card rises (credit top right, "the marker outside the arena today" under it) and
    pushes in slowly.
  - *greatest* (lands): the camera pushes past the marker: the photo blows past while the night vault takes the
    stage from further back, the counter 100 settles above the roof.
  - *scoring*: the roof opens bay by bay from left to right (the ribs stay); the seats light: 4,124 amber dots, the
    empty seats dim. Label "4,124 fans".
  - *night*: the radio at the press table glows, a ring goes out. Label "the radio".
  - *ever,*: a flash in the locker room under the end stands, then an amber point. Label "the flashbulb".
  - *and*: the ball rolls out of the main gate, amber. Label "the ball".
  - *almost* / *nobody* (lands): the camera rises straight up: windows and lamps go out, the labels go, the roof
    shrinks into the town, Chocolate Avenue's Kiss lamps and the stacks slide in and go dark, the town gives way to
    the state map.
  - *saw it.* (ends on): the arena is one tiny amber point on the dark Pennsylvania map; the counter 100 holds above.
- **Sound-off test:** a stranger sees the real roadside marker, then the building at night, opened up, with only a
  handful of tiny amber lights inside it, then the whole state dark with one amber dot under "100". It says: this
  happened, and only these few things saw it.
- **Handovers:** in, the card rises from below while `ball` lifts off (it starts at scene.from - LEAD). Out, the map
  and the counter are lifted off whole by the engine for `cta`.
- **The hook's frame:** HookX1 was still a stub when I built this. I drew the outside vault from the same reference
  (Beato aerial) at the angle the plan names; the camera is one set of numbers in `CloseWitnessesKit.ts`
  (`CW_CAM_A`) so it can be matched to the hook's frame in one place.
