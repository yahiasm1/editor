The Night Wilt Chamberlain Scored 100 and Almost Nobody Saw It

March 2, 1962, in Hershey, Pennsylvania, the chocolate town. Only 4,124 fans showed up, about half the arena. No TV cameras, no New York reporters, and the only recording is radio audio of the fourth quarter.

Wilt had 23 after one, 41 at the half and 69 after three. By then the crowd was chanting "Give it to Wilt!", the Knicks were fouling other Warriors to keep the ball away from him, and the Warriors were fouling right back.

The wildest part? Wilt was a famously bad free throw shooter, and that night he made 28 of 32. Fans poured onto the court and the Warriors won 169 to 147.

Even the famous photo was improvised: the PR man wrote 100 on a piece of paper in the locker room, and a photographer who came to treat his son snapped it. The ball later sold at auction for $551,844.
