Why Wembanyama's 50 Points on 29 Shots Is the Scary Number

Victor Wembanyama scored 50 against the Pelicans on Saturday on 29 shots, 20 of them made. True shooting counts what those points cost: each free throw is 0.44 of a shot, so his 29 shots and 6 free throws come to about 31.5 shooting chances.

50 points on 31.5 chances is a 79% true shooting mark. The league average this season is 57.5%, which turns the same chances into about 36 points. The 14 extra points are the story.

They came at the rim (10 of 12) and from three (5 of 11), in under 32 minutes, in a 114 to 91 Spurs win.
