# GrannyShot (`granny`, dark, 54.02-62.70 s)

"The Lakers hired a free throw coach just for him. Hall of Famer Rick Barry offered to teach him his underhand granny
shot, and Shaq said he'd shoot zero percent before he'd shoot underhanded."

## What I looked at

- `photos/commons-shaq-free-throw-suns-2009-keithallison-ccbysa2.jpg` (1920 x 2871, Keith Allison, CC BY-SA 2.0).
  Shaq square to the line, Suns purple #32 with grey side panels and orange piping, sweat on the scalp, beard, eyes up
  at the rim. The ball (Spalding, NBA logo and signature panel facing us) sits at his right cheek, his right hand
  under it, left hand on its side, fingers spread. His right elbow points down and OUT to the left of the frame (the
  flare a shooting coach would tuck in). The photo ends at the shorts' hem, just above the knees: there are no shins
  or feet. Light from above, front: highlights on the scalp, shoulders, forearm tattoos. Cut-out traced by hand on
  2x gridded crops (no rembg, GrabCut leaked into the dark crowd beside his skin), Chaikin-smoothed, 2 px feather:
  `public/shaq-free-throws/grannyshot-shaq.png` (1200 x 1794; crown y 234, ball centre (412, 581) r 156, right elbow
  (287, 1162)).
- `photos/commons-rick-barry-1976-russreed-pd.jpg` (960 x 1999, Russ Reed, public domain). Barry, Warriors #24, 1976,
  black and white, both arms high in the air, a small hop: the exact shape of an underhand shot's follow-through.
  Kit: white home jersey, "WARRIORS" arched over a circle with the State of California and "24", dark ringed collar
  and armholes, two dark waist stripes, white shorts with "WARRIORS" down a double side stripe, short 1970s inseam,
  white tube socks with two dark rings at the top, white Adidas with three dark stripes, dark wristbands. Shaggy
  70s hair over the ears, sideburns. A referee behind him with a whistle (the reel's referee). Cut-out traced by hand,
  kept grey: `grannyshot-barry.png` (665 x 1911, offset (216, 24) in the photo; crown (334, 332), chin (344, 524),
  soles 1907).
- `photos/commons-rick-barry-warriors-1976-pd.jpg`: the same kit from the side in motion (shorts cut, sock rings,
  shoe stripes, the California map in the circle), used to draw the strobe frames.
- `photos/commons-shaq-free-throw-heat-2005-jannene-ccby2.jpg` fetched now (960 thumb): head cut off at the top, no use.
- Facts (`facts.md`): the Lakers hired Ed Palubinskas, a 90% free throw shooter, as his free throw coach; Barry,
  Hall of Famer, offered the underhand shot; quote "I'd shoot zero percent before I'd shoot underhanded." (Esquire,
  2005). Heights: Shaq 7'1", Barry 6'7".
- How the underhand free throw really moves (Barry's own description): feet shoulder width, ball held at the bottom
  in both hands, fingers spread on the sides, arms straight; bend the knees, keep the head still; rise and swing the
  straight arms up together, release around eye level with backspin, both arms finish high. The photo is that finish.

## Scale (one px/ft for both men)

Head sizes set the scale (Shaq's head 291 px in his PNG = 7.08/8 ft; Barry's 192 px = 6.58/8.2 ft). 104 px/ft,
floor line y 1420: 7'1" hairline y 683, 6'7" hairline y 736. Barry's photo at 0.433 puts his soles on the floor line
by itself (crown to sole 6.56 ft): the photo agrees with the scale. Shaq's photo stops above the knees: both real
photos fade out at the same knee band, and the ink strobe is the one drawn full length down to the floor line.
The ring is not at scale (10 ft would be in the caption): it hangs at the top between them as the target.

## Sound-off test

A tall man in purple with a ball, a hand fixing his elbow; an older man in a 70s Warriors kit, shorter, shooting from
between his knees and swishing; that path offered to the big man and bouncing off him; a card in his words saying
he'd rather shoot zero percent. The story reads without the voice.
