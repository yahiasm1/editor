Hack-a-Shaq: The Player Teams Fouled on Purpose

Shaquille O'Neal was so unstoppable near the rim that teams started fouling him on purpose. At 7 foot 1 he was a wrecking ball under the basket, but at the free throw line he made just 52.7% over his career, 5,935 of 11,252, and missed 5,317.

Dallas coach Don Nelson made the trick famous in 1999. Shaq called him a clown, so at their next game Nelson showed up wearing a clown nose.

Some nights it really worked. On December 8, 2000, Shaq went 0 for 11 at the line against Seattle, still an NBA record. The Lakers hired a free throw coach just for him, and when Rick Barry offered to teach him his underhand granny shot, Shaq said he'd shoot zero percent before he'd shoot underhanded.

Gregg Popovich had his Spurs foul him five seconds into the 2008-09 opener and flashed two thumbs up. Years later the league changed the rules, so an off-ball foul in the last 2 minutes of any quarter now gives the fouled team a free throw and the ball.
