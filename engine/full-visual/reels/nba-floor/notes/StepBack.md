## Sound-off test

A pair of white sneakers standing just inside a painted line steps back over it into a lit gold strip of floor. The
number where they stood is 0.80, where they now stand 1.16, and the multiplication says the step is worth +45%. A
stranger reads: one step behind that line is worth almost half again as much.
