# CornerWatch (scene `cta`, 74.71-82.92 s)

"Next game, watch who's waiting in the corner. Follow for the next number the floor hides, and send this to the
friend who still loves a long two."

## What I looked at (full size)

- `photos/commons-capital-one-arena-playoffs-2017-daniellobo-cc0.jpg` (1280 px) was too soft to push into a corner,
  so I fetched the same Commons file at 3840 x 2560 (`...-cc0-3840.jpg`, saved beside it). What it shows: Capital One
  Arena (still "Verizon Center" on the floor and the ribbon board), 19 Apr 2017, from the upper deck behind one
  baseline, looking up the length of the court. Top third: the rafters, Bullets retired numbers (41 Unseld, 25
  Johnson, 11 Hayes, 10 Monroe), the 1977-78 champions banner, the centre-hung scoreboard with a Chick-fil-A free
  throw promo. A red LED ribbon runs round the bowl. Every seat is full; lots of red shirts. The floor: honey maple,
  the Wizards' darker stain inside both arcs, red lanes, navy out of bounds with "WASHINGTON" across the baseline
  (upside down from this end) and "Verizon Center" / "Wizards" lettering on the floor. Players lined up for a free
  throw at the near basket; the stanchion and backboard cover the middle of the near lane from here. Nobody stands
  in either near corner: the corners are empty at the moment the photo was taken.
- Light: overhead arena light, even, no hard shadows on the floor, slight glare on the lacquer near the far key.
- Scale: the near half court is 966 px wide at the baseline but only ~315 px deep in the 3840 image: a high, distant
  camera, the court strongly foreshortened. Gives a fake away: a sliver or ring drawn flat on top instead of in this
  perspective, or anything that does not sit exactly on the painted line.
- Measured: the four inner corners of the near half court (baseline x sidelines at y = -5.25, half court x sidelines
  at y = 41.75) in the 3840 image: (1461.7, 2277.0), (2427.5, 2266.2), (1568.3, 1961.3), (2308.3, 1952.2). A
  homography from world feet to these four points, drawn back on the photo: the projected three-point line, lane, free
  throw circle, restricted area and 28 ft hash marks land on the real paint within a couple of pixels (checked on an
  overlay). So the gold sliver and every mark can be drawn on that real floor at its true place.
- `profile/ig-ballsimplified-avatar.jpg` (= `context/profile/avatar-*.jpg`): the creator's real avatar, a man on a
  balcony over a night city. Real account details from `context/profile.json`: name "Ball Simplified", bio
  "Basketball Simplified". The follower count is not in facts.md, so it is not on screen.
- `profile/ig-ballsimplified-post-netrating.jpg`: the creator's look (navy cards, hairline, photo credit top right).
- Instagram's own Reels overlay row (from memory of the real app): round avatar, bold username, a white-outlined
  "Follow" button with white type (not the blue profile button: no blue on stage), the paper-plane share glyph.
- Siblings: `ShortestThree` (the close floor, slivers lit, the right corner's 22' 0" point), `LongTwoFades` (its
  homography helpers, the band path, white marks), `PointsPerShot` / `StepBack` (0.80 = 2 x .401).

## The real thing, and where each part comes from

1. The Capital One Arena photo (3840 px, Daniel Lobo, CC0), full stage, pushed from the whole bowl into the near
   right corner of its real floor. Credit top right.
2. On that real floor, in its perspective (the measured homography): a thin white ring on the floor around the
   corner spot, and the gold 21 in sliver between the corner line and the 23'9" circle, labelled "21 in".
3. The creator's card: the real avatar, "ballsimplified", "Basketball Simplified", the Reels "Follow" button.
4. The floor from `close` (same drawing: maple strips, charcoal lane, 2 in white paint, ring and net, both slivers lit),
   small and top-down, then tilted until it lands exactly on the photo's real floor; its boards fade and the real
   floor shows through, the gold slivers lying on it.
5. The long-two band on the real floor; the league's 2025-26 long twos as Basketball-Reference shot-chart marks
   (49 per 1,000 shots: 20 made white dots, 29 missed grey x, .401), the last one popping "0.80".

## Sound-off test

A packed arena, then its empty corner with a gold strip on the floor marked "21 in": that is where to look. A
creator's follow card. The same court the reel measured lands on that real floor, the corners glow, and the few long
twos left are worth 0.80 a shot.
