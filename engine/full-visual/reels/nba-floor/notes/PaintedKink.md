## The real thing this scene shows

The 2 in white three-point paint at the exact point (x 22, y 8.95 ft from the middle of the basket) where the 23'9"
arc stops and the straight corner line starts, on real maple; then the whole floor that forces it.

Every detail that makes it real:
- maple strips 2.25 in wide, each its own shade, staggered end joints, grain only where the camera is close;
- the paint is a band of true width (0.167 ft), with a raised edge: a bright lip on the light side, a hairline shadow
  on the other, only visible at shoe height;
- satin lacquer: a broad light sweep moving across the boards, and long streaks of glare along the boards;
- scuffs (grey arcs from soles) and dust specks, most near the corner and top of the key;
- the lane in charcoal stain, the restricted arc, the free throw circle with its dashed half, lane hash marks, the
  28 ft hash marks on the sidelines, the board and ring from above: all at Rule 1 geometry;
- the apron 3 ft wide in darker stained wood, then navy;
- the tape as in the Lufkin photo, lying on the apron, crank folded, ring end out.

## Sound-off test

A stranger sees one painted line change from a curve to a straight edge, sees it is 14.2 ft straight on both sides,
then sees the floor's two edges lock in right beside it: the straight part is where the floor runs out.
